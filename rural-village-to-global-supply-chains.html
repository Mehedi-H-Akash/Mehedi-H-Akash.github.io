From a Rural Village to Global Supply Chains: My Textile Journey

By Md. Mehedi Hasan Akash · Merchandiser

Growing up as a boy in a rural village, the intricate world of global apparel supply chains felt lightyears away. I was fortunate to have a strong, merit-based educational background, but to be completely honest, I initially had very little interest in the textile sector. It simply wasn't on my radar.

But life has a funny way of steering you toward your true calling.

Slowly, an unexpected curiosity began to take root. I became deeply fascinated by the sheer complexity and engineering of how garments are made. I wanted to understand the entire lifecycle—how a raw, simple fiber is spun, dyed, cut, and transformed into a finished product worn by someone halfway across the world. Driven by this keen desire to learn, and bolstered by my academic foundations—which eventually led me through my Knitwear Industry Management studies at BUFT—I fully committed myself to this dynamic industry.

My professional journey was forged in the fast-paced environments of some of the top-tier organizations in Bangladesh. Working with industry giants like Epyllion Group and Masco Group provided a tremendous learning curve. Those early years were a mixture of everything: immense pressure, incredible teamwork, long hours, and invaluable hands-on experience. Those factory floors taught me the granular realities of apparel manufacturing and the resilience required to thrive in it.

Today, that journey has brought me to a proud new chapter as a Merchandiser at SSTS BD, a multinational liaison office. In this role, I get to bridge the gap between global buyers and local execution, applying every lesson I've learned from the ground up to ensure flawless product delivery.

Looking back, the feeling is entirely mutual: I have given my dedication and hard work to the RMG sector, and in return, it has transformed a rural boy into a global textile professional. It has been a tremendous ride so far, and the passion that started as a small spark continues to drive me forward every single day.
